Privacy Policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

The short version

Coffee Inc 3 does not collect, send, or sell any personal information. Your game lives on your device.

What is stored, and where

  • Your saved game (company name, finances, staff, and so on) is stored in your browser's IndexedDB storage on your device.
  • A few preferences, such as whether the game pauses at the end of each week, are stored in your browser's local storage.
  • The app itself is cached by a service worker so it can open without an internet connection.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no cookies.

Save files you export

When you use Export save, the game creates a file on your device. What happens to that file is up to you. Importing a save reads the file locally; it is never uploaded.

Deleting your data

Choose Settings, then New company, to delete your saved game from this device. You can also clear all data for this site in your browser settings, or remove the app from your Home Screen.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version ever adds something like cloud saves, this policy will be updated first and the change will be explained in the app before anything is sent anywhere.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
